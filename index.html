Game overview: Launch a satellite by adjusting its initial speed and direction. The simulator calculates gravity from the satellite's distance to the planet and updates its motion in real time. Your goal is not to hit the planet or travel the farthest—it is to balance velocity, direction, and gravity.

// PRE-LAUNCH KNOWLEDGE

Three rules. One authentic scientific investigation.

01 / GRAVITY

Gravity points inward

The closer the satellite is to the planet, the stronger the force. Its direction and speed change more rapidly near the surface.

02 / VELOCITY

Velocity shapes the path

The right sideways speed lets the satellite move forward while falling, continually missing the planet's surface.

03 / METHOD

Change one variable at a time

Keep the angle at 0° and test speed first. Once the orbit is nearly stable, fine-tune the direction.
